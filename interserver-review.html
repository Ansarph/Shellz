Shellz quick verdict

A clear monthly option—if you choose the correct InterServer product

Standard Web Hosting is the simplest comparison: the public page showed $2.50 for the first month and $7 per month at renewal, with DirectAdmin. That is easier to budget than a deal that hides the second bill, but it is still a 2.8× jump from the promotional first month.

My buying advice: consider InterServer if monthly flexibility, bundled email, weekly backups, free migration, and DirectAdmin suit you. If you require cPanel, budget for the separate $8 monthly renewal. Treat the $5 Price Lock page as a separate campaign and confirm eligibility in the live cart.

Included features

What InterServer says Standard hosting includes

The items below are provider-reported inclusions, not independent Shellz performance findings.

Website basics

InterServer lists storage, data transfer, email, FTP accounts, free SSL, and one-click application installation on Standard hosting. “Unlimited” usage remains subject to normal-use rules and the provider's terms.

Performance stack

The public technical page lists LiteSpeed, LSCache/QUIC, CloudLinux, SSD caching, RAID-10 storage, and free Cloudflare CDN access. Shellz has not yet benchmarked the result.

Backups and migration

InterServer lists weekly backups and complimentary migration help. Its migration guide says matching cPanel-to-cPanel or DirectAdmin-to-DirectAdmin moves are supported and may take time to begin.

Control panel decision

DirectAdmin can save money; cPanel costs more

Standard Web Hosting uses DirectAdmin. That can be perfectly workable for domains, email, files, databases, and WordPress, but it will feel different if you already know cPanel.

InterServer sells cPanel hosting separately, with a higher public renewal price. Choose the panel before you migrate—not after you discover that a tutorial, backup, or workflow depends on cPanel.

Refund check

InterServer lists a 30-day hosting refund window

InterServer's terms say eligible Standard, Windows, Boost, and WordPress web-hosting purchases can request a full refund within 30 days after signup. After that period, cancelling stops future billing but the amount already paid is generally forfeited.

Important: a money-back period is not permission to skip the cart. Domains, add-ons, taxes, upgrades, dedicated products, and other services can follow different rules. Read the current terms for the exact product you buy.

Decision checklist

Five checks before buying InterServer

1. Match the exact product

Write down Standard DirectAdmin, Price Lock, or cPanel. Do not compare only the first-month number.

2. Capture the cart

Save the plan name, term, amount due today, renewal line, taxes, domain price, and selected add-ons.

3. Confirm the panel

Check whether your current host, migration method, backups, and tutorials work with DirectAdmin or require cPanel.

4. Read resource rules

“Unlimited” is a convenience label, not infinite server capacity. Check fair-use, CPU, inode, email, and abuse rules for your workload.

5. Plan a test

After setup, measure your own page speed, uptime, backups, restore process, email delivery, and support response during the refund window.

FAQ

InterServer review questions

How much does InterServer Standard Web Hosting renew for?

On August 26, 2026, the public Standard page showed $2.50 for the first month and $7 per month at renewal. The 12-month option showed $30 initially and $84 at renewal. Verify the live cart because prices can change.

Is the $5 Price Lock offer the same as Standard hosting?

No. InterServer presents the $5 Price Lock offer on a separate campaign page. Its terms say the rate remains while an eligible account stays on that plan, but domains, add-ons, taxes, upgrades, and separate services can differ.

Does Standard hosting include cPanel?

No. The Standard page checked by Shellz uses DirectAdmin. cPanel hosting is a separate product that showed an $8 monthly renewal.

What does “unlimited” mean on InterServer hosting?

InterServer publicly lists unlimited storage, transfer, email, and several account features for normal website use. It should not be read as unlimited computing resources or permission for prohibited storage and abuse. Check the current terms for your workload.

Has Shellz independently tested InterServer?

Not yet. This page is based on current official public sources. Shellz has not published a checkout screenshot, speed benchmark, uptime log, dashboard walkthrough, restore test, or timed support test for InterServer.
