A2 Hosting rebrand

What changed from A2 Hosting to Hosting.com?

A2 Hosting announced on April 28, 2025 that it had become Hosting.com. An earlier official announcement said World Host Group acquired A2 Hosting in January 2025. Hosting.com described the new name as a global-brand transition while continuing its hosting products and support.

What is clear

Hosting.com is the successor brand to A2 Hosting. Buyers searching for an A2 review should now verify information on Hosting.com.

What not to assume

Do not assume an older A2 plan name, discount, renewal, dashboard or included feature is unchanged.

Best next check

Match a legacy A2 product against the current plan and ask whether panel, migration and billing terms differ.

Refund check

The 30-day guarantee does not cover everything

Hosting.com says eligible shared, WordPress, email and reseller hosting purchased directly can receive a hosting refund within 30 days. Domains, renewals and transfers, VPS, dedicated servers, SSL certificates and some third-party products are excluded or handled separately.

Renewal warning: the guarantee is for qualifying new purchases. Confirm cancellation timing before the next invoice is generated or charged.

FAQ

Hosting.com review questions

Is Hosting.com the same as A2 Hosting?

A2 Hosting announced in April 2025 that it had become Hosting.com. Check current plans instead of assuming an older A2 plan is unchanged.

How much does Hosting.com renew for?

It depends on the plan, region and term. On August 26, 2026, the global homepage displayed one Starter example at $35.88 for the first 12 months and $143.88 for the following 12 months.

Does Hosting.com provide free migration?

The provider says managed migrations are included across plans. Confirm exact scope for your platform and destination plan.

What does the money-back guarantee exclude?

The provider lists exclusions or separate treatment for domains, renewals, transfers, VPS, dedicated servers, SSL certificates and some third-party products.

Has Shellz tested Hosting.com speed, uptime or support?

No. Shellz checked official pricing and policy pages but has not published a checkout capture or hands-on service test.
