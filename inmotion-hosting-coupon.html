Checkout checklist

Verify the promotion before entering payment

1. Match the exact product

Shared, WordPress, reseller, VPS and dedicated offers use different routes. Confirm the selected product and plan name.

2. Record the billing term

A low monthly equivalent can require a longer upfront payment. Save the full amount due today.

3. Save the renewal line

Record the regular hosting renewal, domain renewal and any add-on renewal separately.

4. Check backups and migration

Automatic backups can be an add-on, and complex migrations may fall outside the free scope.

Refund warning

A guarantee does not refund every charge

Shared Hosting and Hosting for WordPress are listed with a 90-day guarantee. Other product and term combinations can use shorter windows. Domain fees, add-ons and professional services are non-refundable, and a domain registration fee can be deducted after using a free domain credit.
