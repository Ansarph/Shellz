InMotion Hosting Review 2026: Price, Renewal & Refund

Page reviewed: August 29, 2026 · Prices, discounts and plan terms can change.

InMotion Hosting is worth comparing for small-business hosting when you want cPanel, email, migration help and a longer eligible refund window. The main buying risk is long-term cost: introductory monthly equivalents can rise sharply at renewal, and automatic backups may be an extra purchase.

Shellz quick verdict

A practical small-business option with a serious renewal check

The strongest reasons to shortlist InMotion are cPanel, provider-listed migration help, bundled SSL and email, multi-site plan options and a 90-day refund window for eligible Shared and WordPress hosting. It is less attractive if your priority is the lowest long-term bill or automatic backups included by default.

Main warning: compare the total due today with the full renewal invoice. Do not judge value from the monthly-equivalent introductory price alone.

Buyer fit

Who should buy—and who should avoid

Consider InMotion Hosting

  • You want cPanel and email on a small-business hosting plan.
  • You need migration help for a standard cPanel or WordPress site.
  • You value a longer eligible refund window.
  • You may grow from shared hosting into VPS or dedicated infrastructure.

Compare alternatives first

  • You need the lowest long-term price, not just a discounted first term.
  • You require automatic backups included without another subscription.
  • You want a monthly plan with little or no renewal increase.
  • You need independently tested performance before choosing.

Features and limits

What matters beyond the headline price

Useful inclusions

  • NVMe storage on current shared plans
  • Free SSL and cPanel email
  • Free domain credit on eligible 12-, 24- or 36-month terms
  • Provider-listed website migration
  • cPanel, SSH, Git and common development tools

Important watch-outs

  • Domain renewal starts after the first-year credit.
  • A domain fee can be deducted from an eligible refund.
  • Automatic backups are described as an optional add-on for shared plans.
  • Free migration scope is simplest for standard cPanel or WordPress transfers.
  • Plan limits and support channels vary by tier.

Migration

Free transfer does not mean every migration is unlimited

InMotion's product pages advertise free website migration. Its support guidance says most free transfers involve a cPanel backup or one standard WordPress site within applicable limits. Large, unusual or complex moves may require professional migration assistance.

Before switching: send support the source host, control panel, site count, total storage, databases, email accounts and any custom server requirements. Ask for the free-versus-paid scope in writing.

Backup warning

Budget for a restore-ready backup plan

InMotion's shared-hosting guide calls automatic backups an optional add-on. Its Backup Manager documentation describes off-server backups and a base storage allowance after purchase. Some plans may include backup storage, but buyers should not assume the schedule, retention or capacity without checking the selected cart.

Refund policy

The 90-day guarantee has product and fee limits

InMotion lists a 90-day guarantee for Shared Hosting and Hosting for WordPress. It also applies to six-month or longer VPS, UltraStack One for WordPress and Reseller terms. Eligible month-to-month VPS, UltraStack One, Reseller and Dedicated plans use a 30-day window. Add-ons, domain fees and professional services are non-refundable.

Domain warning: if a free domain credit was used, InMotion says the standard registration fee is deducted from the hosting refund.

FAQ

InMotion Hosting review questions

Is InMotion Hosting good for beginners?

It can suit beginners who want cPanel, migration help, email, SSL and human support. Compare the total due, renewal and backup cost before choosing.

How much does InMotion Hosting renew for?

It varies by plan and term. On August 29, 2026, one Launch route displayed $4.99 per month equivalent and $13.99 per month at renewal. This is not a Shellz checkout capture.

Does InMotion include automatic backups?

Its shared-hosting guidance describes automatic backups as an optional add-on. Verify whether your exact plan includes storage and what it costs to renew.

What is the refund period?

Shared Hosting and Hosting for WordPress are listed with a 90-day window. Other product and term combinations can use 90, 30 or 7 days, with non-refundable fees and services.

Has Shellz tested InMotion performance?

No. The current review is based on official public information and clearly separates that from unpublished checkout and service testing.
