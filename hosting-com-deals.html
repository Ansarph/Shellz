FAQ

Hosting.com deal questions

Does Hosting.com have a public coupon code?

The official promotions page did not display a reusable typed code when Shellz checked it on August 26, 2026. It linked to current deals instead.

Can I use an old A2 Hosting coupon?

Do not assume a legacy code remains valid after the rebrand. Treat it as unverified unless the current Hosting.com cart accepts it for your exact plan and term.

Do promotional prices renew at the same rate?

No. Hosting.com's official promotions page says promotional pricing applies to the first term only and renews at the regular rate.

What offer is shown for Managed WordPress?

The official Managed WordPress page describes a low-price first-month trial and two months free with an annual subscription. Confirm the live eligibility and total before buying.
