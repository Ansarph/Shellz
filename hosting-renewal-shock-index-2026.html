Hosting Renewal Price Shock Index 2026: 7 Real Carts

Shellz compared seven captured hosting carts with confirmed renewal lines or displayed regular prices. Five examples share a 12-month first term, making their monthly-equivalent price jumps easier to compare before a buyer commits.

See the indexDownload CSVView screenshots

Headline finding

Five comparable 12-month examples averaged a 293.8% renewal-price uplift

Across the five 12-month first-term examples, the monthly-equivalent renewal or displayed regular price averaged about 3.94× the captured intro equivalent. The median uplift was 305.1%, with observed values ranging from 206.1% to 372.9%.

Methodology

The primary index uses five Shellz checkout captures with a 12-month first term: Hostinger Premium, HostGator Hatchling, ScalaHosting Mini SPanel, GreenGeeks Ecosite Lite 25 and SiteGround StartUp. The captures were recorded July 12–14, 2026.

Monthly-equivalent increase is calculated as: ((renewal monthly equivalent ÷ captured intro monthly equivalent) − 1) × 100.

Shellz labels each row by evidence type. A confirmed renewal line means the renewal amount or rate was explicitly visible in the captured checkout. An estimated regular-price renewal means Shellz calculated the next-term equivalent from the regular price displayed in the checkout; it is not presented as a separately confirmed future invoice.

Taxes, optional add-ons, regional pricing, plan changes and future provider price changes can alter actual charges. This research compares the specific captured examples only.
